Rabbit, the Giraffe Walks Past way

Rabbit from the archive, made the way Giraffe Walks Past was made. SAM 2.1 traced the rabbit in every frame. The rig is the chosen Quick, Draw! doodle itself (quickdraw:6204342979788800): its own lines, bent at a few joints, so every frame is the same drawing. 20 keys were placed by eye over the silhouettes; in-betweens follow the silhouette frame by frame. Scenery is real Quick, Draw! doodles on depth planes that slide with the measured camera; the silhouette is painted as a cel.

Round 2 · bar_metNamed “rabbit”; motion broken. The animated rabbit is a mirror of the doodle (faces right instead of left) and its body is rounder and more hunched than the doodle's long low body. The ears keep the doodle's two pointed loops, but they often cross the head line or float loose from it, which the doodle's single continuous outline does not do.

Wine-bottle, the Giraffe Walks Past way

Wine-bottle from the archive, made the way Giraffe Walks Past was made. SAM 2.1 traced the wine-bottle in every frame. The rig is the chosen Quick, Draw! doodle itself (quickdraw:5665738343841792): its own lines, bent at a few joints, so every frame is the same drawing. 11 keys were placed by eye over the silhouettes; in-betweens follow the silhouette frame by frame. Scenery is real Quick, Draw! doodles on depth planes that slide with the measured camera; the silhouette is painted as a cel.

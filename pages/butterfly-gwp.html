Butterfly, the Giraffe Walks Past way

Butterfly from the archive, made the way Giraffe Walks Past was made. SAM 2.1 traced the butterfly in every frame. The rig is the chosen Quick, Draw! doodle itself (quickdraw:5895381575532544): its own lines, bent at a few joints, so every frame is the same drawing. 15 keys were placed by eye over the silhouettes; in-betweens follow the silhouette frame by frame. Scenery is real Quick, Draw! doodles on depth planes that slide with the measured camera; the silhouette is painted as a cel.

Round 1 · bar_metNamed “a butterfly perched with its wings folded upward”; motion broken. The doodle has both wings spread wide on either side of the body, while the animation shows them pressed together into one narrow upright shape. The lobed wing outline, the segmented diagonal body with tick marks, and the looped antennae are kept.
Round 2 · bar_metNamed “a butterfly with two lobed wings, a long segmented body, a round head and antennae”; motion smooth. The animated butterfly keeps the doodle's lobed wing outlines, the ladder-marked body line that extends past the wings, the small circle head and the two antennae, only made smaller and narrower.
Round 3 · bar_not_metNamed “a butterfly perched on a person's fingertips (character: butterfly; scenery: hand)”; motion smooth. Only the looped antennae and dashed body come from c.png; the wings are a new pointed, double-lined shape instead of the doodle's rounded lobed single-line wings.
Round 4 · bar_metNamed “a butterfly (side view, wings folded) perched on a fingertip”; motion broken. The wing outline with its notched lobe and the looped antennae come from the doodle, but the doodle's long body with tick marks is reduced to a short doubled line and is missing entirely in frames 1-2.
Round 5 · bar_metNamed “A butterfly with closed wings perched on a fingertip”; motion smooth. The character is the doodle's lobed wing, curled antennae and tick-marked abdomen seen from the side with wings closed; the short doubled line along the wing's leading edge (visible in frames 4, 5, 13, 16) is close to the doodle's own double-line habit but is slightly heavier than the doodle uses there.

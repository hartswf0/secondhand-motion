Ant, the Giraffe Walks Past way

Ant from the archive, made the way Giraffe Walks Past was made. SAM 2.1 traced the ant in every frame. The rig is the chosen Quick, Draw! doodle itself (quickdraw:5421013154136064): its own lines, bent at a few joints, so every frame is the same drawing. 13 keys were placed by eye over the silhouettes; in-betweens follow the silhouette frame by frame. Scenery is real Quick, Draw! doodles on depth planes that slide with the measured camera; the silhouette is painted as a cel.

Round 1 · bar_metNamed “an ant (three round body segments, six legs, two antennae) standing on the ground”; motion smooth. The animated ant uses the same three loose round segments, short connecting dashes, straight stick legs and two antenna strokes as the doodle, mirrored to face right, with the head drawn somewhat smaller.
Round 2 · bar_metNamed “Two ants standing head to head on the ground, touching antennae”; motion smooth. The left ant uses the doodle's construction (small head with two antennae, large middle oval, rear oval, straight stick legs) mirrored to face right, but its segments are rounder and taller than the doodle's flat ovals, and the right ant is a smaller variant.
Round 3 · bar_metNamed “Two ants meeting head to head, drawn as the doodle's ant (round abdomen, larger thorax oval, small pentagon head, stick legs and antennae); the left ant is the doodle mirrored, the right ant a scaled-down copy.”; motion broken. The left ant reads as the doodle mirrored, but the right ant is drawn at about half the size, which makes it read as a smaller ant rather than a second ant of the same size as in the film.
Round 4 · bar_metNamed “Two ants (a larger left ant and a smaller right ant) meeting head to head”; motion smooth. Both ants read as the doodle's three-part ant with its pentagonal head and stick legs, though the right ant's rear oval is drawn smaller than the doodle's rear oval and the heads blur together into overlapping polygons at frame 14.

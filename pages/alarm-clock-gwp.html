Alarm-clock, the Giraffe Walks Past way

Alarm-clock from the archive, made the way Giraffe Walks Past was made. SAM 2.1 traced the alarm-clock in every frame. The rig is the chosen Quick, Draw! doodle itself (quickdraw:5502112672776192): its own lines, bent at a few joints, so every frame is the same drawing. 16 keys were placed by eye over the silhouettes; in-betweens follow the silhouette frame by frame. Scenery is real Quick, Draw! doodles on depth planes that slide with the measured camera; the silhouette is painted as a cel.

Review

Round 1 · bar_metNamed “a twin-bell alarm clock with two hands and two small feet, sitting on a shelf”; motion smooth. The animated clock uses the same open circle, two arc bells, two straight hands with a center dot and two short angled feet as the doodle, so it reads as the same drawing.
Round 2 · bar_not_metNamed “a doodled double-bell alarm clock, handled by a human hand and arm”; motion broken. The doodle is one circle with bells, but the animation draws a second full body circle behind it, so the character is not the same single-circle drawing set in motion.
Round 3 · bar_metNamed “A doodled twin-bell alarm clock (round face with two hands, two bells, two legs) turned by a hand.”; motion broken. The face-on clock is the c.png drawing, but the turned pose in frames 5-10 adds a large rectangular side body that is not in the doodle's construction and reads as a different object.
Round 4 · bar_metNamed “An alarm clock with two bells and two feet, turned around and back by a hand.”; motion broken. The clock is the c.png doodle in the frontal frames, but in the turned frames 5-10 its bells and face are redrawn as overlapping loops that the doodle's sparse, gapped single-stroke style does not contain.

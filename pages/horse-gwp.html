Horse, the Giraffe Walks Past way

Horse from the archive, made the way Giraffe Walks Past was made. SAM 2.1 traced the horse in every frame. The rig is the chosen Quick, Draw! doodle itself (quickdraw:4658862839824384): its own lines, bent at a few joints, so every frame is the same drawing. 20 keys were placed by eye over the silhouettes; in-betweens follow the silhouette frame by frame. Scenery is real Quick, Draw! doodles on depth planes that slide with the measured camera; the silhouette is painted as a cel.

The construction matches the doodle (zigzag mane, pointed ears, scribbled dot eye, open jaw curve, two neck lines), but one ear is drawn as a sideways pointed shape sticking out of the neck line rather than upright as in the doodle.
Round 3 · bar_metNamed “a horse's head and neck, with a jagged mane, two pointed ears, an eye and a jaw line”; motion broken. The animated head uses the doodle's parts (zigzag mane, two leaf-shaped ears, scribbled eye, cheek curve with jaw and throat lines) in the same single-stroke style, but it is mirrored to face right and drawn with a long vertical face instead of the doodle's short wedge-shaped muzzle.
Round 4 · bar_not_metNamed “A horse's head and neck (a horse lowering its head to eat).”; motion broken. The mane is redrawn as a top zigzag, then a cluster, then vertical tufts instead of the doodle's one zigzag line running down the back of the neck, and the frontal oval head with two eyes in frames 1-4 and 13-16 is a construction the doodle does not contain.
Round 5 · bar_metNamed “a horse's head and neck (mane, two ears, one or two eyes, muzzle)”; motion broken. The profile frames 5-10 read as the doodle's construction (scribble eye, leaf ears, jaw hook, zigzag mane) mirrored to face right, but the front-view frames 1-4 and 13-16 use a long oval face that the doodle never draws, and the ears in 13-16 become crossed tangled strokes unlike the doodle's two clean ear loops.
Round 6 · bar_metNamed “a horse's head and neck (horse at a fence lowering its head to eat)”; motion broken. The profile frames carry the doodle's zigzag mane, open pointed ears, chin hook and plain strokes, but the frontal views use smooth U-curves and squiggle eyes where the doodle uses straight angular segments and a single filled dot eye.

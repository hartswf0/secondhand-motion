Duck, Drawn Keys over SAM

Duck from the archive, redrawn the way Giraffe Walks Past was made, starting from drawn poses. SAM 2.1 traced the duck in every frame. The four keys an image model drew in the chosen doodle's identity, each approved by a critic, were traced to strokes, split from their scenery by the silhouette and moved onto it; in-betweens match strokes key to key and follow the silhouette frame by frame. The scenery is drawn once, and the silhouette is painted as a cel under the character.

Round 1 · bar_not_metNamed “a duck or duckling standing upright, seen from the front, with webbed feet on a ground line”; motion broken. The reference is a side-view duck made of a few long straight polyline segments with a pointed beak, a single sloping body outline and stick legs ending in small triangle feet. The animation is a detailed frontal trace with smooth curved lines, wing outlines, chest marks, broad webbed feet and a ground line, so it does not share the reference's construction or stroke habits.

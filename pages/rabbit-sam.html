Rabbit, Drawn Keys over SAM

Rabbit from the archive, redrawn the way Giraffe Walks Past was made, starting from drawn poses. SAM 2.1 traced the rabbit in every frame. The four keys an image model drew in the chosen doodle's identity, each approved by a critic, were traced to strokes, split from their scenery by the silhouette and moved onto it; in-betweens match strokes key to key and follow the silhouette frame by frame. The scenery is drawn once, and the silhouette is painted as a cel under the character.

Hourglass, the Giraffe Walks Past way

Hourglass from the archive, made the way Giraffe Walks Past was made. SAM 2.1 traced the hourglass in every frame. The rig is the chosen Quick, Draw! doodle itself (quickdraw:5880797120692224): its own lines, bent at a few joints, so every frame is the same drawing. 17 keys were placed by eye over the silhouettes; in-betweens follow the silhouette frame by frame. Scenery is real Quick, Draw! doodles on depth planes that slide with the measured camera; the silhouette is painted as a cel.

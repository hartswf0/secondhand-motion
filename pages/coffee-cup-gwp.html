Coffee-cup, the Giraffe Walks Past way

Coffee-cup from the archive, made the way Giraffe Walks Past was made. SAM 2.1 traced the coffee-cup in every frame. The rig is the chosen Quick, Draw! doodle itself (quickdraw:5699704845238272): its own lines, bent at a few joints, so every frame is the same drawing. 50 keys were placed by eye over the silhouettes; in-betweens follow the silhouette frame by frame. Scenery is real Quick, Draw! doodles on depth planes that slide with the measured camera; the silhouette is painted as a cel.

Round 1 · bar_not_metNamed “A plastic measuring cup with a spout, drawn as the doodle cup (rim with three hatch lines, body with crossing lines).”; motion broken. The doodle is an open-topped tapered cup with an oval rim holding three vertical hatch lines and two horizontal body lines, but the animation draws a closed rounded blob whose rim is a vertical pair of strokes for the first 21 frames and whose tapered body and narrow base are never shown.
Round 2 · bar_metNamed “A plastic measuring cup (the doodle's cup with three strokes inside the rim and horizontal strokes on the body), held by a grey hand.”; motion broken. The cup keeps the doodle's rim strokes and body strokes, but because it is scaled up and cropped the body is mostly cut off, so in e1-e7 and e41-e50 it reads more like a rim or bowl than the full cup in c.png.
Round 3 · bar_metNamed “A plastic cup (measuring cup) of grains being tilted and emptied, seen from above.”; motion smooth. When seen from above (e.png 1-24, 41-50) the rim is drawn as a large dome rather than the doodle's flat ellipse, so in those frames the cup reads partly like a helmet; frames 25-40 match the doodle most closely.

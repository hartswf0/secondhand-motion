Basket, the Giraffe Walks Past way

Basket from the archive, made the way Giraffe Walks Past was made. SAM 2.1 traced the basket in every frame. The rig is the chosen Quick, Draw! doodle itself (quickdraw:6147165976002560): its own lines, bent at a few joints, so every frame is the same drawing. 23 keys were placed by eye over the silhouettes; in-betweens follow the silhouette frame by frame. Scenery is real Quick, Draw! doodles on depth planes that slide with the measured camera; the silhouette is painted as a cel.

The basket keeps the doodle's open rim, angular bowl and single-line handle throughout, though when the handle is edge-on it becomes a thin two-line loop that the doodle never shows, which is a necessary consequence of the rotation.
Round 2 · bar_metNamed “A woven basket with one arched handle, turning on its handle.”; motion smooth. The basket keeps the doodle's single-stroke rim, hooked rim ends and single arched handle; the only departure is that in the end-on frames the handle becomes a thin pinched loop, which the doodle never shows but which is a reasonable rotation of it.

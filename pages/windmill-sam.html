Windmill, Drawn Keys over SAM

Windmill from the archive, redrawn the way Giraffe Walks Past was made, starting from drawn poses. SAM 2.1 traced the windmill in every frame. The four keys an image model drew in the chosen doodle's identity, each approved by a critic, were traced to strokes, split from their scenery by the silhouette and moved onto it; in-betweens match strokes key to key and follow the silhouette frame by frame. The scenery is drawn once, and the silhouette is painted as a cel under the character.

Round 1 · bar_not_metNamed “a windmill seen from the front, shown as four sails around a central hub”; motion broken. The reference has a tall rectangular tower outline behind the sails, a small oval hub, long narrow petal-shaped sails with no crossbars, and a door mark at the bottom; the animation has no tower outline and draws rounded paddle sails with a crossbar near each tip. In frames 5-8 two of the sails are not closed shapes at all, only open edge lines and short spikes, which the reference never does.

Dragon, the Giraffe Walks Past way

Dragon from the archive, made the way Giraffe Walks Past was made. SAM 2.1 traced the dragon in every frame. The rig is the chosen Quick, Draw! doodle itself (quickdraw:6190476665290752): its own lines, bent at a few joints, so every frame is the same drawing. 13 keys were placed by eye over the silhouettes; in-betweens follow the silhouette frame by frame. Scenery is real Quick, Draw! doodles on depth planes that slide with the measured camera; the silhouette is painted as a cel.

The animated drawing uses the same polygon head, zigzag back, curled tail and open leg strokes as the doodle, so it reads as the same drawing.

The animated character keeps the doodle's polygon head, zigzag back spikes, curled tail and straight-line legs, so it reads as the same drawing; it does not take on the film's horned head, open jaw or claws.
Round 3 · bar_metNamed “A spiky-backed cat-like or dragon-like creature with a curled tail (stand-in for the film's horned dinosaur), drawn in the doodle's polygon-head, zigzag-spine style.”; motion smooth. The character reads as the c.png drawing; only the face stroke and front ear change shape slightly from frame to frame more than the doodle would by moving.

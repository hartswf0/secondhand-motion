Cannon, the Giraffe Walks Past way

Cannon from the archive, made the way Giraffe Walks Past was made. SAM 2.1 traced the cannon in every frame. The rig is the chosen Quick, Draw! doodle itself (quickdraw:5140965633294336): its own lines, bent at a few joints, so every frame is the same drawing. 32 keys were placed by eye over the silhouettes; in-betweens follow the silhouette frame by frame. Scenery is real Quick, Draw! doodles on depth planes that slide with the measured camera; the silhouette is painted as a cel.

The side views are the c.png doodle turned in space, but the front views (e.png 1, 28-31) break it into two separate ovals beside the wheel, which the doodle's construction does not contain.
Round 3 · bar_metNamed “A cannon (field gun on a wheel)”; motion smooth. In the end-on frames (e1, e15-e16, e30-e31) the barrel becomes a narrow upright capsule with the wheel reduced to a line, which is less recognisable as the c.png drawing than the side views, though it keeps the same strokes.

Traced Keys

The 40 selected drawings were stills drawn by an image model. Tracing turns each one into pen strokes, the same kind of data a Quick, Draw! doodle is, so a key can be in-betweened, put on a depth plane and measured. Grey strokes stay in place across the subject's four keys; dark strokes change.

What the numbers say

Recall is the share of the drawing's ink that a traced line covers. Ink pieces kept counts whole marks, so a missing eye shows up even when recall is high. Precision is 1.0 by construction and is not shown.

What the layers say

On a locked-off shot most background strokes should stay put. They do not: the image model redrew the trees, bowl and ground on every key. The fix is upstream: one background plate per shot, the character drawn on blank for each key.

What is still missing

The four keys do not share a topology, so they cannot be in-betweened yet. Next is fitting the rig to each trace, as the giraffe shot does over SAM silhouettes.

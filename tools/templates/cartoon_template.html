Giraffe test: drawn vs traced

The second giraffe is drawn, not traced. An agent placed a pose on five keyframes (outlined in red in the filmstrip), a doodle rig turned each pose into Quick, Draw!-style strokes, and the frames between were interpolated and held to the SAM mask.

Quick, Draw! classifier confidence on the five drawn keyframes. Real human giraffe doodles score 0.75 on the same classifier.

rewardLooks hand-drawn44 vs 36

Typicality percentile of stroke statistics against 4,971 real giraffe doodles, drawn frames vs traced frames.

rewardHolds the footage pose0.66

Mask fit across all 33 frames. Keyframes reach 0.76; the weakest in-betweens (frames 15 and 31) drop to 0.47.

criticBlind review

A separate agent, shown shuffled A/B pairs, preferred the drawn version in 4 of 5 keyframes: bar met on drawing language and consistency, partial on pose. Its main gap, stiff legs with no gait, was repaired with knees and re-placed hooves.

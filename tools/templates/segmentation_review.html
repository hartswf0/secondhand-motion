How these views line up

The left and middle panels show the same 12 fps CI sample. Its time is approximate because the CI snapshot did not retain decoded source timestamps. Known samples that cross into another shot are blocked from drawing pairing. The cartoon seeks to its nearest recorded source time and stays paused while scrubbing. Open the comparison player for normal-speed playback. Samples outside the retained cartoon interval have no paired drawing.

The model’s IoU estimate is a model output, not a measured drawing-to-source fit. Segmentation can miss thin parts or merge several objects. These masks do not establish recognition, motion quality, or training eligibility.

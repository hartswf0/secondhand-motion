Giraffe Walks Past

One archive shot redrawn as a multiplane cartoon. Six planes of doodles, real Quick, Draw! drawings and agent drawings in the same idiom, sit at different depths and move with the camera pan measured from the footage, so near planes slide faster than far ones. The walking giraffe is rotoscoped from nine keys drawn over SAM silhouettes, and its silhouette is painted as a cel so it hides what is behind it.
